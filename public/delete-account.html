Permanent Data Deletion

Deleting your VocabClash account permanently removes all personal profile information, LP rank, matchmaking stats, win streaks, and Mistake Vault saved questions from our Firestore database.

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

You can instantly delete your account and all data directly within the VocabClash app:

  1. Open VocabClash on your device or browser.
  2. Click your Aspirant Profile icon (top right or bottom navigation).
  3. Click ⚖️ Legal & Account (below Help & Aspirant Support).
  4. Select Delete Account and confirm your request.

Method 2: Email Deletion Request

If you no longer have access to the application, you can submit a manual deletion request to our Data Protection Officer:

  • Send an email to: support@vocabclash.in
  • Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request
  • Include the registered email address associated with your VocabClash account.
  • Requests are processed and all cloud records purged within 7 business days.

Data That Is Permanently Removed

  • Account Details: Email, display name, profile avatar, authentication UID.
  • Game Progress: Total LP, tier rank, match history, head-to-head records.
  • Study Vault: Mistake Vault question bookmarks and personalized accuracy logs.
